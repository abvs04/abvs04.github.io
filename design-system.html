Ember Glass

The design system behind this portfolio: smoked glass over a fire-lit dusk. Deep amber-brown sky, floating ember-lit shapes, and soft panels in the spirit of Apple’s dark materials, with ember orange kept for everything you can act on. All values live in css/tokens.css, and components only use tokens.

Principles

Four rules every new component has to follow.

  1. Orange is for doing. Ember orange marks actions, the selected filter, focus, the arrow on every card, and the one highlighted word in the headline. Information stays neutral.
  2. Content sits on glass. Panels are smoked glass with a heavy blur, a faint warm 1px edge and a deep shadow, so the scene shows through without fighting the text.
  3. Big, soft, round. Panels use 20–24px radii, buttons and tags are full pills, and shadows are tinted warm, never grey.
  4. One big moment. The story showcase is the page’s single show-piece: five chapters, each told in three beats (problem, what I did, result), with real video cards so footage always plays. Everything else moves only when someone hovers, focuses or opens something, and reduced-motion settings swap the showcase for a plain list.
